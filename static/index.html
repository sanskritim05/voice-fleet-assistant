      How reports are assessed

      1. A language model talks with the driver, asks a follow-up question when a report is vague, and acts through tools: logging the incident, alerting dispatch, looking up the truck.
      2. Safety rules audit every decision. They can raise a severity or force an escalation, but never lower one, and critical reports are never held up by questions.
      3. If the model can't be reached, the rules answer on their own.
